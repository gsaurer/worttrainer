# Worttrainer

Private spelling practice with paper answers and adult-reviewed results.

## Language

**Parent account**:
An adult's sign-in identity and the owner of a private word collection and its
practice history. Separate registrations do not share collections.
_Avoid_: Shared installation account, family membership

**Account practice data**:
A parent account's language preferences, word collection, practice attempts, and
saved rounds, including recognized handwriting. It does not include sign-in credentials.
_Avoid_: Account metadata

**Word collection**:
The correct spellings a parent account has chosen to practice, grouped by
language and current learning category.

**Enabled language**:
A language a parent account has chosen for adding words and starting new rounds.
Disabling it keeps its existing words, progress, and saved rounds.

**Default language**:
The enabled language selected initially when adding a word or setting up practice.
Each parent account chooses its own default.

**Practice attempt**:
A parent-confirmed correct or incorrect result for one word. Adding a word,
listening to dictation, and reading a photo are not practice attempts.

**Practice round**:
An ordered, numbered set of words with a recorded start and writing position.
All words are dictated before a parent reviews the answers manually or with
photo-reading suggestions. Only confirmed results count toward round statistics.
The round and any recognized handwriting belong to the parent account.
_Avoid_: Photo round, test score for the child

**Learning category**:
New, +, or −: a word's current practice position, determined by confirmed
attempts rather than a grade for the child.
